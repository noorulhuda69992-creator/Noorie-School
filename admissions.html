Admission process

A simple, parent-friendly process

1

Review information

Check eligibility, required documents, fee placeholders and important instructions.

2

Complete the form

Download the form or use the online admission form page to prepare a printable application.

3

Submit documents

Provide the completed form and required supporting documents to the school office.

4

School review

The school reviews the application and contacts the parent/guardian regarding next steps.

5

Confirmation

Complete the applicable admission and fee formalities after receiving school confirmation.

Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the class being applied for and the school's current admission policy.

  • Student should meet the age/class requirements set by the school.
  • Previous academic record may be reviewed where applicable.
  • Admission is subject to available seats and school approval.
  • Parents/guardians should provide accurate information.
